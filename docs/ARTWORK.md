## Business collection expansion

Eight additional illustrations cover business strategy, budget planning, cash flow,
investment research, asset allocation, business partnership, financial reporting
and long term planning. Strategy appears on About, budgeting on Pricing and cash
flow beside the referral calculator. The remaining images are reusable assets for
future business and educational content; they do not introduce an investment
product to Assignwork.

## Art direction

All eight supplied images were treated as visual references, not instructions. The workshop informed team planning; the blue and lavender groups informed community and learning; the laptop group informed task artwork; the hands informed payment transfers; the binocular illustration informed career outlook; and the two finance collections informed wallet, profile and progression artwork.

The collection uses fine ink outlines, expressive adult figures, lime `#d4f717` accents and light grey backgrounds. Travel scenes retain natural colours. No reference watermark or artist signature is reused.
